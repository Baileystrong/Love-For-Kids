About us

Love in action.

Love for Kids was created around a simple idea: when a community comes together, children can receive the encouragement and resources they need to keep moving forward.

Our mission

Our mission is to support children and families through educational resources and practical community assistance. Our focus may include students in grade 5 and above who need items such as learning technology and school materials.

Our commitment

We aim to communicate honestly about our work, our current status, and how support is used. Before contributing, supporters should review the information provided on this website and ask questions if anything is unclear.

Important: Love for Kids should not be described as a registered nonprofit or 501(c)(3) organization unless and until that status has been officially obtained.
